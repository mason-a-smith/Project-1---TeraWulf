## Historical normalization and forecast architecture

The [normalization catalog](evidence/NORMALIZATION_AND_CONTAMINATION.md) reconciles FY2025 revenue, balance sheet and cash flow to the filing, then bridges reported EBIT−186.213 to normalized−150.709 by identifying accelerated depreciation, remeasurement and disposal items. Ordinary depreciation and compensation remain expenses. Warrant fair-value changes are below EBIT, and debt-funded cash is not FCFF.

The forecast does not compound a mining-heavy base revenue. Average billable capacity and inferred first-year escalating rent produce HPC revenue. Power is reimbursed at cost. Mature site margin, overhead, working capital, construction transfers, recurring maintenance and debt costs flow into all three statements. CIP is not depreciated before commissioning. Cash shortfalls draw only against an explicit assumed limit. Remaining deficits stay visible and invalidate valuation.

Every year includes a complete income statement, balance sheet and cash-flow statement. Retained earnings move through net income; old awards and warrant reclassification are identified equity movements; cash equals prior cash plus CFO+CFI+CFF. Asset sales remove the investee and create receipts; old construction accruals consume cash without creating PP&E again. These are simplified economic schedules rather than a claim to forecast every GAAP line or covenant. Static ROU/other-account balances, approximate debt amortization and omitted monthly cash troughs remain limitations.

The most consequential assumption is $5,208.636m remaining construction:$1,198.636m Lake plus $4,010m Justified. Assigning all $2,371.364m opening CIP against Lake is unverified; applying Lake's $10m/MW upper guidance to Kentucky is a separate judgment. Reconciliation to project budgets, paid invoices, unpaid commitments and tenant contributions is the first diligence request. The engine cannot cure unsupported inputs by balancing.

## Valuation engine and causal cases

FCFF = economic EBIT−unlevered cash taxes+D&A−growth capex−maintenance−working-capital investment−prepaid-rent consumption−legacy construction/remediation settlements. Borrowing, repayments and interest are excluded. The forecast cash flow retains negative construction years. Discount times are0.5,1.5,…5.5 years fromJune30, using the inherited end-period half-year convention.

WACC base10% is a judgment:40% equity at15.44% plus60% debt at8.5% after25% tax. It is not a fresh measured CAPM estimate or market leverage. With no immediately usable tax shield, the same mix implies11.28%; the sensitivity spans8–14%. Project guarantees are not automatically guarantees of common equity. Terminal nominal growth spans0–2%, with replacement spending equal to depreciation plus amortization of contract costs and working-capital growth funded. Perpetuity assumes renewal/reletting beyond finite contracts and is therefore a material assumption.

Base explicit FCFF PV−$3,357.9m plus terminal PV $3,870.5m produces operating EV $512.6m. Add unrestricted cash less $100m reserve and PV of net asset-sale receipts; deduct opening principal, minority buyout and acquisition claims once. The uncapped equity residual is−$2,453.5m. Terminal PV is755% of net operating EV because explicit construction PV is negative; this is a warning about dependence on long-run economics, not an arithmetic bug.

The favorable case combines lower construction cost(−10%), higher rent realization(+5%),85% mature site margin and8% WACC; early ramp margins are unchanged. It produces $2.74/share. The adverse case combines20% cost overrun,5% rent haircut,75% mature margin and12% WACC; it fails funding. A one-year delay with unchanged capex timing also fails. These correlated operating narratives are distinct from the one-at-a-time tests.

Because the quote and capital bridge dates differ, this is a price-hurdle exercise—not a claim that the market literally forecasts that amount or a particular MW count.

## External methods and sensitivity interpretation

The [peer policy](evidence/PEER_POLICY.md) admits CIFR and qualifies IREN by operating fit before calculation. All prices areOctober16, balances and basic sharesJune30, and revenueTTMJune30. EV/revenue and P/book are calculated with disclosed definitions. Loss-period P/E is rejected. Transaction evidence informs only the asset-sale bridge; no lifetime lease headline is treated as acquisition EV.

Peer EV/revenue produces an unfloored−$2.28 to $7.76 WULF share diagnostic; P/book gives $0.72–$1.95. These methods disagree because revenue is transitional, book equity is distorted by accumulated losses/warrants, and peers differ in GPU ownership, financing, growth and asset maturity. With two peers, leave-one-out simply selects the other endpoint. No methods are averaged.

Broad-range feasible value sensitivities rank WACC, mature margin, rent, overhead and collections. Capex and maintenance upper endpoints fail funding and cannot be assigned valid value spans. Narrow feasible ranges reorder the drivers to WACC, maintenance, capex, margin, rent, overhead and collections. Thus the ranking is partly a choice of input ranges. Mature margin leads final-year EBIT; maintenance leads cumulative FCFF in the fully feasible narrow band. Debt rate affects levered income/liquidity, while FCFF stays unchanged at fixed WACC.
